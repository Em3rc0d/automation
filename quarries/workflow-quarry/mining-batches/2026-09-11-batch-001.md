### A-01 Invoice Generator from Form

- path: `workflows/04-invoice-generator-from-form/workflow.json`
- blob SHA: `8f9185da7c72b9129b49a7496d28528485c767ca`
- target family: Quote2Cash / INVOICE_GENERATE
- gate: INSPECTED
- trigger: n8n Form Trigger
- providers/nodes: Form Trigger, Set, PDFShift HTTP API, Gmail, Google Sheets
- side effects: generates PDF, emails customer, appends record to Sheet
- strong ideas:
  - simple end-to-end request → PDF → email → log flow;
  - clear customer-facing form contract;
  - reusable separation of computed invoice fields.
- blockers before hardening:
  - invoice number uses random suffix and is not a durable/idempotent sequence;
  - duplicate submission can create/send duplicate invoice;
  - hardcoded USD presentation and company copy;
  - Google Sheet is acting as ledger/control state;
  - no approval policy for high-value invoice;
  - no ProcessRecord / ExecutionEvent / SavingsEvent / Incident integration;
  - no retry/partial-failure compensation (e.g. email succeeds but Sheet write fails).
- decision: P0 hardening candidate.

### A-04 Autonomous Email Triage Agent

- path: `workflows/09-autonomous-email-triage-agent/workflow.json`
- blob SHA: `69fa77d4a911ffff58e63951c975ede53f29a1bf`
- target family: OpsFlow / EMAIL_CLASSIFY_ROUTE / EMAIL_DRAFT_REPLY
- gate: INSPECTED
- trigger: Gmail unread inbox polling
- providers/nodes: Gmail Trigger, n8n AI Agent, OpenAI, memory, subworkflow tools, Google Calendar HTTP, Google Sheets
- side effects: Gmail label; draft creation via subworkflow; Sheets log
- strong ideas:
  - explicit rule: draft replies for human review; never auto-send;
  - calendar availability must be checked before suggesting times;
  - thread-level memory key.
- blockers before hardening:
  - label and draft tools are placeholder subworkflow IDs;
  - AI agent has broad autonomous tool authority without our Action/Approval contract;
  - logging to Google Sheets instead of ProcessRecord/AuditEvent;
  - no schema-defined classification result;
  - no idempotency/duplicate-email event guard;
  - full email body may reach model/logs without PII minimization policy;
  - hardcoded model (`gpt-4o`) and polling cadence.
- decision: P0 hardening candidate; preserve human-review principle.

- providers/nodes: n8n AI Agent, OpenAI, memory, Clearbit HTTP, CRM subworkflow, booking subworkflow

  - BANT-inspired information gathering;

  - agent can invoke external side effects without platform Action/Approval enforcement;

- decision: P0 pattern source, but harden as deterministic workflow + optional AI extraction/scoring rather than unrestricted agent.
